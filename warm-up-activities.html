🎃 Halloween Warm-up Activities 🎃

Unit 6: Requests with "Will" and "Would you mind?"

Level: B1 Beginner | Duration: 5-10 minutes each

WARM-UP ACTIVITY 1
🎭 Halloween Vocabulary Charades

Objective: Students practice scary/spooky vocabulary through mime and guessing, while using "Will you..." requests.

Level: B1 Beginner

Group Size: Whole class or small groups

Duration: 7-10 minutes

📋 Instructions:

  1. Divide students into 2-3 groups.
  2. One student from each group acts out a scary/spooky word WITHOUT speaking (scary, frightening, spooky, creepy, eerie, haunted, evil, terrifying, mysterious, dark, sinister, creepy-crawly).
  3. Other groups have 30-45 seconds to guess the word.
  4. Language practice: When guessing, students should use: "Will you give us a hint?" or "Will you show us again?" (teacher or actor can respond with "Will you guess...?" or "Yes, I will!").
  5. Each correct guess earns 1 point. Group with most points wins!

📚 Vocabulary to Act Out:

1. Scary (Show fear/shock)
2. Frightening (Look terrified)
3. Spooky (Move mysteriously)
4. Creepy (Crawl slowly)
5. Eerie (Make ghostly sounds)
6. Haunted (Float like a ghost)
7. Evil (Look wicked/laugh)
8. Terrifying (Extreme fear)
9. Mysterious (Hide, peek out)
10. Dark (Close eyes, move carefully)
11. Sinister (Evil smile)
12. Creepy-crawly (Act like insects)
💬 Language Examples:

Student 1: "Will you give us a hint?"

Teacher/Actor: "Yes, I will. It's something very scary!"

Student 2: "Is it 'frightening'?"

Teacher/Actor: "Yes, you're right! I will give you a point!"

💡 Teacher Tips:

• Encourage students to ask for hints using "Will you...?" questions

• Write the vocabulary words on cards and randomly select them

• Can play with hand gestures only or full-body movements

• Make it competitive to increase engagement

WARM-UP ACTIVITY 2
🎤 "Would You Mind?" Dialogue Practice

Objective: Students practice polite requests using "Would you mind?" and respond with "Yes, I will" or "No, I won't" in Halloween context.

Level: B1 Beginner

Group Size: Pairs

Duration: 5-7 minutes

📋 Instructions:

  1. Students work in pairs (Student A and Student B).
  2. Distribute scenario cards with Halloween situations (see below).
  3. Student A makes a polite request using "Would you mind...?" or "Will you...?"
  4. Student B responds with "Yes, I will" or "No, I won't" + reason.
  5. Students switch roles after each scenario.
  6. Practice 3-4 scenarios per pair, or rotate pairs for more practice.

🎭 Halloween Scenario Cards:

Scenario 1 - Exploring a Haunted House:

A: "Would you mind turning on the light? It's very dark."
B: "Yes, I will. I'm frightened too."

Scenario 2 - At a Halloween Party:

A: "Will you turn down the music? It's too spooky!"
B: "No, I won't. I love the eerie sounds!"

Scenario 3 - Decorating:

A: "Would you mind helping me put up this terrifying skeleton?"
B: "Yes, I will. Where should we put it?"

Scenario 4 - Trick-or-Treating:

A: "Will you come with me to that creepy house?"
B: "No, I won't. That place looks sinister!"

Scenario 5 - Scary Movie Night:

A: "Would you mind closing the curtains? There are creepy-crawlies outside."
B: "Yes, I will. I don't want mysterious shadows in here."

💬 More Language Examples:

Example 1:
A: "Would you mind helping me find this evil painting?"
B: "Yes, I will. I'll help you look for it."

Example 2:
A: "Will you turn off that frightening movie?"
B: "No, I won't. I enjoy the terrifying parts!"

Example 3:
A: "Would you mind looking at this dark corner with me?"
B: "Yes, I will, but only if you go first!"

💡 Teacher Tips:

• Model the dialogue first before students start

• Emphasize the difference between "Would you mind...?" (polite) and "Will you...?" (direct request)

• Encourage natural responses and creativity

• Have advanced students add more details to their responses

• Record some pairs to review pronunciation and intonation later
