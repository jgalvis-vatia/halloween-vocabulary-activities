📖 Reading Activity: "The Mysterious Mansion"

Last Halloween, Maria and her friends decided to explore an old house on the edge of town. The local people said it was haunted, and many spooky stories were told about it.

When they arrived at the gate, Maria said to her friend Tom, "Will you come with me to the front door?" Tom looked at the dark building and replied, "Yes, I will, but would you mind turning on your flashlight? It's very eerie here."

Inside, they saw creepy shadows on the walls and heard strange sounds. "Will you turn on the lights?" asked Maria nervously. Her friend Sarah responded, "The switches are sinister-looking and covered in creepy-crawly insects!"

As they explored, they discovered a locked door. "Would you mind helping me open this door?" Maria asked. Tom said he would, but he was also frightened. Finally, they found a collection of old paintings with terrifying faces and evil expressions. It was the most scary moment of their adventure!

But when they turned on their flashlights properly, they realized the paintings were just old art. "Will you forgive us for being so mysterious discoveries?" laughed Maria. The friends will definitely turn down any future invitations to haunted mansions!

💡 Language Focus:

Notice how the characters use "Will you...?" and "Would you mind...?" to make requests. Also observe the two-part verbs like "turn on," "turn off," and "turn down" used in context.

✅ Comprehension Activity: Multiple Choice
Read the questions and select the correct answer:

1. Why did Maria and her friends want to explore the old house?

2. What did Tom ask Maria to do?

3. What did the friends find that scared them the most?

4. How did the story end?

5. Which phrase shows a polite request?

📚 Vocabulary Matching Activity
Match the words with their meanings:
Word Definition
Eerie Extremely frightening or scary
Sinister Strange and frightening; mysterious
Creepy Causing an uncomfortable feeling of fear
Terrifying Evil or wicked; suggesting harm or danger
Spooky Causing fear or a feeling of mystery
